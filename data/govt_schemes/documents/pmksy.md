# PMKSY (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY) has been formulated with the vision of extending the coverage of irrigation ('Har Khet Ko Pani') and improving water use efficiency ('Per Drop More Crop') in a focused manner with end-to-end solution on source creation, distribution, management, field application, and extension activities.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Micro Irrigation Subsidies**: Focuses on drip and sprinkler irrigation systems. 
*   **Small and Marginal Farmers**: Subsidies of up to **55%** of the cost of installing drip or sprinkler systems are provided.
*   **Other Farmers**: Subsidies of up to **45%** of the cost are provided to other category landholding farmers.
*   **Water Harvesting**: Subsidies for construction of farm ponds, check dams, and water harvesting structures.
*   **Energy Efficiency**: Support for installation of solar water pumps and energy-efficient motor pumpsets.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Farmers**: All landholding farmers, members of cooperative societies, self-help groups, and FPOs.
*   **Tenant Farmers**: Tenant farmers who have registered tenancy agreements for at least 7 years are eligible.
*   **Water Resources**: Access to a water source (borewell, well, canal, or pond) on the cultivable land.
*   **State Coverage**: Active across all states, including Telangana, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Haryana, Punjab, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Pattadar Passbook / Land Ownership Certificate / ROR.
*   Borewell / Water Source proof.
*   Soil and Water Test Report (from authorized laboratory).
*   Electricity Bill or connection certificate for pumpsets.
*   Quotation for Drip/Sprinkler system from registered supplier.
*   Bank Passbook copy.

## Applicable Crops
*   **Horticultural Crops**: Vegetables, Banana, Mango, Citrus Fruits, Papaya, Pomegranate.
*   **Cash Crops**: Sugarcane, Cotton, Mulberry.
*   **Field Crops**: Maize, Pulses, Groundnut.

## Scheme Category
*   Irrigation & Water Conservation.
