# FPO Scheme (Formation & Promotion of 10,000 FPOs)

## Overview
The Central Sector Scheme for the "Formation and Promotion of 10,000 Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)" was launched to aggregate small, marginal, and tenant landholding farmers. Grouping farmers into FPOs enhances their bargaining power, allows collective procurement of quality inputs (seeds, fertilizers) at bulk discounts, enables sharing of advanced machinery, and helps negotiate better market prices for their collective harvests.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Equity Grant Support**: Matching equity grant of up to **₹2,000 per member** (subject to a maximum limit of **₹15 Lakh per FPO**) to strengthen the financial base of the FPO.
*   **Credit Guarantee Fund**: Credit guarantee cover of up to **₹2 Crore per FPO** for loans taken from banks, ensuring collateral-free credit access for infrastructure development.
*   **Management Cost Subsidy**: Financial support of up to **₹18 Lakh per FPO** spread over 3 years to cover salary of CEO, accountant, office rent, and utility expenses.
*   **Capacity Building**: Free training and skill development programs for FPO directors and executives.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **FPO Formation**: A minimum of **300 farmer members** is required in plain areas (100 members in hilly or Northeast regions) to form an eligible FPO.
*   **Farmer Profile**: At least **50% of the FPO members** must be small, marginal, or landless farmers.
*   **Registration**: Must be registered as a Producer Company under the Companies Act or as a Cooperative Society under the Cooperative Societies Act.
*   **State Coverage**: Active in all Indian states including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Incorporation Certificate of the FPO (Company / Cooperative).
*   PAN Card and TAN Card of the FPO.
*   Aadhaar Cards and land ownership details of all registered farmer members.
*   Audited financial statements and list of directors.
*   Bank Account details of the FPO.
*   Business Plan / Detailed Project Report (DPR).

## Applicable Crops
*   All agricultural, horticultural, organic crops, and allied products (Dairy, honey, fisheries, livestock).

## Scheme Category
*   Farmer Groups & Collective Aggregation.
