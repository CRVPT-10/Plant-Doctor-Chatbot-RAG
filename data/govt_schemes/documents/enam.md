# eNAM (National Agriculture Market)

## Overview
National Agriculture Market (eNAM) is a pan-India electronic trading portal which networks the existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. The portal provides single-window services for all APMC related information and services including commodity arrivals, prices, buy/sell trade offers, and provision for electronic payment directly into farmers' bank accounts.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Transparent Price Discovery**: Farmers can view real-time prices across various mandis to sell their produce at the best rate.
*   **Direct Payments**: Buyers pay directly into the farmer's bank account via online banking/UPI (DBT), eliminating middlemen commissions.
*   **Quality Assaying**: Provides free assaying (quality testing) of produce in mandis to fetch premium prices for high-quality crops.
*   **Reduced Transaction Cost**: Reduces transaction costs and information asymmetry between buyers and sellers.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Farmers, Traders, Commission Agents**: Any farmer, FPO, trader, or commission agent registered with local APMC mandis can register on eNAM.
*   **Registration Cost**: Registering on the eNAM portal is **100% free**.
*   **State Coverage**: Networks mandis across states including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Haryana, Tamil Nadu, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Bank Account Details (linked with Aadhaar) with Cancelled Check / Passbook.
*   Mobile Number (for SMS alerts on trade bids and payments).
*   Mandi Registration Card (if registered in an APMC).

## Applicable Crops
*   All agricultural and horticultural commodities (Paddy, Cotton, Wheat, Maize, Potatoes, Onions, Spices, Fruits, Oilseeds, Pulses, etc.) listed on the eNAM platform.

## Scheme Category
*   Agricultural Marketing & Digital Trade.
