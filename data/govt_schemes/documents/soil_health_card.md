# Soil Health Card Scheme

## Overview
The Soil Health Card (SHC) scheme is promoted by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. A Soil Health Card is a printed report that is handed over to a farmer for each of his landholdings. It contains the status of his soil with respect to 12 parameters: N, P, K (Macro-nutrients); S (Secondary-nutrient); Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B (Micro-nutrients); and pH, EC, OC (Physical parameters). Based on this, the card also indicates fertilizer recommendations and soil amendment suggestions for the farm.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Free Soil Testing**: Soil sample collection, testing, and card distribution are provided **100% free of cost** to the farmers.
*   **Fertilizer Recommendations**: Provides precise dosage recommendations for chemical fertilizers and organic manures to improve yield and save costs.
*   **Cycles**: Cards are issued once every **2 years** for all landholdings so that changes in soil health can be monitored.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **All Farmers**: All landholding farmers across India are eligible to receive Soil Health Cards for their respective fields.
*   **State Coverage**: Implemented in all states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, etc.).

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Land Ownership Passbook / Pattadar Passbook details (to link coordinates of the field).
*   Active Mobile Number (to receive soil status notifications).

## Applicable Crops
*   All crops (Paddy, Cotton, Wheat, Sugarcane, Vegetables, Spices, Pulses, Fruits, etc.) as the soil testing provides generic crop suitability recommendations.

## Scheme Category
*   Soil Health & Fertilizer Management.
