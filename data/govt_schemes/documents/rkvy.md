# Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY)

## Overview
Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY) is a state-plan scheme initiated to ensure holistic development of agriculture and allied sectors. The scheme incentivizes states to increase public investment in agriculture and allied activities, providing flexibility and autonomy in planning and executing customized schemes for local cropping systems and farm dynamics.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Infrastructure Support**: Subsidies of up to **50%** for building customized cold storages, drying yards, custom hiring centers for machinery, and sorting units.
*   **Farm Mechanization**: Subsidies of up to **40% to 50%** for procurement of tractors, power tillers, rotavators, harvesters, and seed drills (specifically targeting women, SC/ST, and small/marginal farmers).
*   **Allied Sector Development**: Direct grants and loans for establishing dairy units, sheep/goat rearing houses, poultry farms, and fish ponds.
*   **Agri-Entrepreneurs Support**: Under 'RKVY-RAFTAAR', funding and incubation support is provided to agricultural startups and innovators.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Target Beneficiaries**: Individual landholding farmers, SC/ST farmers, women farmers, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
*   **Allied Sectors**: Dairy, poultry, fishery, and sericulture farmers are also eligible.
*   **State Coverage**: Implemented across all states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, West Bengal, etc.).

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Pattadar Passbook / Land Ownership certificate / LPC.
*   Bank Account details (linked with Aadhaar).
*   Project quotation or invoice copy of the machinery/infrastructure from authorized dealers.
*   SC/ST Caste Certificate (if claiming higher subsidies).
*   Passport size photo.

## Applicable Crops
*   All crops (Paddy, Cotton, Sugarcane, Maize, Vegetables, Wheat, Pulses, Millets, etc.) and allied activities (Fisheries, Sericulture, Dairy, Poultry).

## Scheme Category
*   Farm Mechanization & Allied Sectors.
