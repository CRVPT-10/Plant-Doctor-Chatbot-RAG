# National Horticulture Mission (NHM)

## Overview
The National Horticulture Mission (NHM) is a sub-scheme of the Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH). It aims to promote holistic growth of the horticulture sector (including fruits, vegetables, root and tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, and bamboo) through area-based regionally differentiated strategies.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Establishment Subsidy**: Subsidies of up to **40% to 50%** of the cost for establishing new orchards (Mango, Guava, Citrus, Sapota, Pomegranate) and vegetable gardens.
*   **Polyhouse & Greenhouses**: Subsidies of up to **50%** for construction of protected cultivation structures (Polyhouse, Shade Net house, Greenhouse) to grow high-value vegetables and flowers.
*   **Quality Planting Material**: Subsidies for setting up small and model nurseries to produce certified high-yielding grafting plants.
*   **Post-Harvest Cold Chain**: Support for establishing cold storage units, packhouses, refrigerated vans, and ripening chambers.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Farmers**: Individual farmers, group of farmers, FPOs, self-help groups, and cooperative societies.
*   **Landholding**: Must have cultivable land suitable for horticultural crops.
*   **State Coverage**: Active in all covered districts under MIDH across states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh, etc.).

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Pattadar Passbook / Land Ownership documents / ROR.
*   Soil and Water testing feasibility report.
*   Detailed Project Report (DPR) for high-value infrastructure projects (like cold storage or large polyhouses).
*   Supplier quotation for polyhouse materials / saplings.
*   Bank Account passbook copy.

## Applicable Crops
*   **Fruits**: Mango, Banana, Guava, Pomegranate, Grapes, Citrus, Papaya.
*   **Vegetables**: Tomato, Capsicum, Cucumber, Exotic Vegetables.
*   **Flowers**: Rose, Gerbera, Carnation, Marigold.
*   **Spices**: Turmeric, Ginger, Chilli.

## Scheme Category
*   Horticulture Development.
