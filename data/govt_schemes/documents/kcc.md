# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

## Overview
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme is designed to provide farmers with timely and hassle-free short-term credit from the banking system. It meets the financial requirements of cultivation, purchase of agricultural inputs, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing, consumption requirements of farmer households, and maintenance of farm assets and activities allied to agriculture.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Flexible Short-term Loan**: Offers flexible credit limits to buy seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and hire farm machinery.
*   **Low Interest Rates**: Base interest rate is **7%** per annum.
*   **Prompt Repayment Incentive**: Government provides a **3% interest subvention** (subsidy) for prompt repayment, reducing the effective interest rate to **4%** per annum!
*   **No Collateral up to ₹1.6 Lakh**: No collateral (security/mortgage) is required for loans up to **₹1.6 Lakh** (which can be extended up to ₹3 Lakh under certain conditions).
*   **Crop Insurance Integration**: Farmers holding KCC are automatically eligible for crop insurance coverage under PMFBY.
*   **Validity**: KCC is valid for **5 years** with an option to extend based on credit history.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Cultivators**: All farmers who are individuals/joint borrowers and owner-cultivators.
*   **Tenant/Sharecroppers**: Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers.
*   **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)**: SHGs or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers.
*   **Allied Activities**: Farmers engaged in Animal Husbandry, Poultry, Dairy, and Fisheries are also eligible.
*   **State Coverage**: Issued by all commercial banks, RRBs, and cooperative banks across all states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, etc.).

## Documents Required
*   KCC Application Form.
*   Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / PAN Card (Identity Proof).
*   Address Proof (Utility Bills / Aadhaar).
*   Land records (Pattadar Passbook / ROR / Revenue department extracts).
*   Cropping pattern certificate (Sowing report).
*   Passport size photograph.
*   Bank Account statement for 6 months.

## Applicable Crops
*   All crops (Paddy, Cotton, Sugarcane, Wheat, Maize, Horticultural crops, etc.) and allied activities (Fisheries, Dairy, Poultry).

## Scheme Category
*   Agricultural Credit & Loans.
