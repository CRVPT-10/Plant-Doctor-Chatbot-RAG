# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a flagship Central Sector scheme launched by the Government of India to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families across the country. The scheme aims to supplement the financial needs of farmers in procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Direct Income Support**: ₹6,000 per year is provided to eligible farmer families.
*   **Installment Mode**: The amount is paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every four months.
*   **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**: The funds are transferred directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries, ensuring transparency.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Eligible Farmers**: All landholding farmer families in the country who own cultivable land in their names are eligible.
*   **Small and Marginal Farmers**: Small and marginal farmers with landholdings are primary beneficiaries.
*   **State Coverage**: Active in all Indian states including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, Rajasthan, etc.

## Exclusions (Ineligible Categories)
*   Institutional landholders.
*   Families where one or more members belong to the following categories:
    *   Former or current holders of constitutional posts.
    *   Former or current Ministers, Members of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha/State Legislative Assemblies/Councils.
    *   Former or current Mayors of Municipal Corporations, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
    *   Active or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government, Ministries, Offices, Departments, and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).
    *   All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more (excluding multi-tasking staff/Class IV/Group D employees).
    *   Persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
    *   Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card (compulsory).
*   Landholding Ownership Documents / Pattadar Passbook / Land registry papers showing ownership of cultivable land.
*   Active Bank Account details (linked with Aadhaar).
*   Active Mobile Number.

## Applicable Crops
*   All crops (Paddy, Cotton, Wheat, Sugarcane, Maize, Pulses, Vegetables, etc.) as the income support is generic.

## Scheme Category
*   Income Support & Financial Aid.
