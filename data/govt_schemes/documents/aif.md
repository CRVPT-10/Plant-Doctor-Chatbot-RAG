# Agri Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

## Overview
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) is a medium-to-long term debt financing facility for investment in viable projects for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets. It aims to mobilize capital for setting up warehouses, cold chains, processing units, sorting and grading units, and custom hiring centers.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Interest Subvention (Subsidy)**: Subvention of **3% per annum** on all loans taken under this scheme up to a limit of **₹2 Crore** for a maximum period of **7 years**.
*   **Credit Guarantee Cover**: Credit guarantee coverage is available under the CGTMSE scheme for loans up to **₹2 Crore** without requiring collateral.
*   **Eligible Entities**: Farmers, FPOs, Agri-entrepreneurs, startups, primary agricultural credit societies (PACS), and cooperative societies.
*   **Refinance Support**: Accessible through commercial banks, cooperative banks, RRBs, and NBFCs.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Borrowers**: Individual landholding farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Agricultural Startups, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), and Agri-Entrepreneurs.
*   **Target Infrastructure**: Post-harvest assets (cold chain, warehouses, packaging units, assaying labs) or community assets (smart farming sensors, bio-stimulant units, custom hiring centers).
*   **State Coverage**: Implemented nationwide including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Punjab, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Detailed Project Report (DPR) / Business Plan.
*   Aadhaar Card and PAN Card of the applicant (or registration proof of the entity).
*   Land registry papers / ROR / Lease agreement (minimum 10 years lease for leased lands).
*   Supplier quotation for machinery and civil works.
*   Financial projections and bank loan application form.
*   Bank Account passbook copy.

## Applicable Crops
*   All agricultural and horticultural crops, value-added products, and organic farm produce.

## Scheme Category
*   Agri Infrastructure & Post-Harvest.
