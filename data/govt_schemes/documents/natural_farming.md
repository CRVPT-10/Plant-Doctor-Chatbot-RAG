# National Mission on Natural Farming

## Overview
The National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF) is a central sector scheme launched to promote chemical-free, non-synthetic, and livestock-based natural farming across India. The scheme focuses on restoring soil health, decreasing cultivation costs, improving farmer incomes, reducing environment pollution, and promoting safe, chemical-free food for consumers.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Direct Financial Assistance**: Eligible farmers receive financial assistance of **₹15,000 per hectare** over 3 years to cover the cost of preparing natural formulations (Jeevamrit, Beejamrit) and purchasing natural farming inputs.
*   **Bio-input Resource Centers**: Subsidies of up to **₹2.25 Lakh** to establish village-level Bio-input Resource Centers (BRCs) for bulk preparation and distribution of natural pesticides and fertilizers.
*   **Soil Testing Support**: Free soil health monitoring tests are conducted before and after transition to record improvement in organic carbon content.
*   **Certification Support**: Free farmer group certification (PGS-India) to label products as "Natural Farming Produce" for premium price bidding.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **Farmers**: Individual landholding farmers interested in transitioning from chemical farming to natural farming.
*   **Clustering**: Farmers forming clusters of a minimum of **50 hectares** (approx. 50-100 farmers) are prioritized.
*   **Livestock Access**: Must have access to indigenous cows/livestock to prepare cow-dung and cow-urine-based formulations.
*   **State Coverage**: Promoted across all states, including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Land registry passbook / Pattadar Passbook showing ownership of cultivable land.
*   Bank Account details (linked with Aadhaar).
*   Consent Letter agreeing to completely stop using chemical fertilizers and synthetic pesticides.
*   Indigenous Cow registration or verification certificate (if available, or verified by local veterinary officer).

## Applicable Crops
*   All crops (Paddy, Cotton, Wheat, Maize, Millets, Spices, Vegetables, Fruits, Spices, etc.).

## Scheme Category
*   Natural & Organic Farming.
