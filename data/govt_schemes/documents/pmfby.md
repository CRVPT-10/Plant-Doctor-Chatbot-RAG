# PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is the government-sponsored crop insurance scheme that integrates multiple stakeholders on a single platform. It aims to support sustainable production in the agriculture sector by providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage arising out of unforeseen events, stabilizing the income of farmers to ensure their continuance in farming, and encouraging farmers to adopt innovative and modern agricultural practices.

## Benefits and Subsidies
*   **Low Premium Rates**: The premium rate is extremely low for farmers:
    *   **Kharif Crops**: Maximum 2% premium of the sum insured paid by farmers.
    *   **Rabi Crops**: Maximum 1.5% premium of the sum insured paid by farmers.
    *   **Horticultural/Commercial Crops**: Maximum 5% premium paid by farmers.
*   **Government Subsidy**: The balance premium is heavily subsidized by the Central and State governments on a 50:50 basis (or 90:10 in Northeast states).
*   **Coverage Range**: Covers yield losses (standing crops), prevented sowing/planting, post-harvest losses, and localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, natural fire).

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **All Farmers**: All farmers including sharecroppers and tenant farmers growing the notified crops in the notified areas are eligible.
*   **Non-Loanee Farmers**: Voluntary for non-loanee farmers.
*   **Loanee Farmers**: Voluntary for loanee farmers who have taken crop loans (previously mandatory, now optional).
*   **State Coverage**: Active across participating states such as Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, etc.

## Documents Required
*   Aadhaar Card.
*   Land Records (ROR / Pattadar Passbook / Land Possession Certificate) or tenancy/sharecropping agreement.
*   Sowing Certificate issued by local agricultural department officers or Patwari.
*   Bank Account details (linked with Aadhaar).
*   Cancelled Check / Bank Passbook copy.

## Applicable Crops
*   **Food Crops**: Cereals, Millets, Paddy, Wheat, Maize, Coarse Cereals.
*   **Oilseeds**: Groundnut, Mustard, Soybean, Sunflower.
*   **Annual Commercial/Horticultural Crops**: Cotton, Sugarcane, Potato, Onion, Ginger, Turmeric, Chillies, Banana, Mango.

## Scheme Category
*   Crop Insurance & Risk Mitigation.
